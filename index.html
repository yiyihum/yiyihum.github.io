Hi, I'm Yimi 👋

I am a PhD student at UC San Diego, advised by Prof. Jingbo Shang. In 2026, I am a Student Researcher at Google DeepMind. I previously interned at TikTok and Microsoft Research Asia.

I build LLM agents that can assess themselves, keep improving, and make scientific discoveries. My work spans three directions:

  • Introspection and agent control: hidden-state signals, self-assessment, and value modeling, used to steer long-horizon tasks.
  • Agent learning and evolution: reusing tools and experience, and revising knowledge over time.
  • Evaluation and scientific discovery: separating task performance from genuine discovery ability, and benchmarking code and data agents.
